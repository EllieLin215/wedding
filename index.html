OUR STORY

其實也沒有什麼驚天動地的浪漫劇情，就是突然發現，只要有你在身邊，原本普通的日子都會閃閃發光。
謝謝我們選擇了彼此，
成為這輩子最合拍的玩伴，一起探索世界
誠摯邀请最親愛的你們，
來見證我們邁入人生下一關的幸福時刻！
Nothing overly dramatic, no movie-like romance—just the simple realization that with you around, even the ordinary days start to shine.
Thank you for choosing me, and for being my favorite partner-in-crime for life.
We warmly invite you, our loved ones, to witness and celebrate the start of our next big chapter!
